# Governance logo assets

`linux-foundation-europe-white.svg` is the official white Linux Foundation Europe
logo, downloaded unchanged from the [Linux Foundation Europe website](https://linuxfoundation.eu/):
[original SVG](https://linuxfoundation.eu/hubfs/lf-Europe-white%201.svg).

The white logo is used on the dark opening and closing slides. ApeiroRA and
NeoNephos retain their existing official assets.
